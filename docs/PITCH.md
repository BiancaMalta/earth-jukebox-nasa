# Pitch & submission (Owner: Narr.)

## High-Level Summary (EN)

Earth Jukebox is an accessible browser experience that pairs seven NASA Earth visualization sequences with sound generated as the visitor plays them. People can follow a variable over time, inspect image-derived readings, focus on a region or country, and experiment with a timestamp-aligned mix of multiple variables. Fixed, documented mappings make the sound repeatable; keyboard and screen-reader support make the experience usable without relying on the map alone. Every estimate is identified as image-derived, with its coverage and scientific limits explained.

## 30 s video script

NASA Earth visualizations show how our planet changes, but a map is not accessible to everyone. Earth Jukebox lets you choose from seven NASA visualizations and hear each frame as the data changes. Follow temperature, wind, precipitation, humidity, or air-quality patterns; move through time; explore a region; or compare several layers. The mappings are documented, and image-derived estimates come with clear limits. Earth Jukebox makes the same planetary story available through sight and sound.
