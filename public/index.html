About the values and methods

Read the frame

What the numbers mean

Without a regional selection, readings summarize decoded pixels across the image. Select a country group or country to focus the map and switch both readings and sound to that region. Pixel inclusion shows the share of analyzed image pixels used, not accuracy or geographic surface area.

One phenomenon, one sound identity

The supplied temperature value maps to one note. Normalization limits are unavailable.

Dates keep their supplied time zone. If none is given, the player says so.

How these image estimates are calculated

Python converts image colors to the nearest value in the visualization legend. Logos and timestamps are excluded. Regional estimates use pixel centers inside the selected boundaries; country groups follow whole-country assignments and contain land territories, not surrounding oceans. Europe includes all Russia; Asia excludes Russia.

P90 is the unweighted 90th percentile of decoded pixel values, not a confidence level. These remain image-derived estimates; weighting cannot reconstruct missing or rejected pixels and does not make them direct numerical GEOS-FP fields.

Pixel inclusion = pixels used ÷ analyzed pixels after excluding overlays. It does not measure scientific accuracy, instrument reliability or geographic surface area. Missing inclusion is unavailable, not zero; 100% inclusion does not establish accuracy.

Python computes norm = (mean − legend minimum) ÷ (legend maximum − legend minimum), limited to 0–1 and rounded to three decimals. It is a position on the fixed legend scale, not a percentile or a percentage of Earth. The front displays the supplied values. Only temperature uses norm directly for sound; the other instruments use mean in its physical unit.

Keyboard guide

Use Tab and Shift + Tab to move between controls. Use Enter or Space to activate buttons.

  • SpacePlay or pause when focus is outside controls.
  • ← →Previous or next frame when focus is outside controls.
  • ← →Adjust a slider when it has focus.

Pause to explore the readings with a screen reader. Automatic playback does not announce every frame; manual frame selection announces its values.

Optional spoken dates

Browser speech is optional and depends on available voices. It is separate from screen-reader support.

Speak dates reads the current date immediately, then every N frames during playback, even without music. Disable sound silences speech too.
